# Writing & profile — Lucas Arana

> Notes on engineering teams, hiring, AI and operations connecting Latin America with US companies.

Canonical page: https://lucasarana.com/writing/
Last updated: 2026-09-19

Lucas Arana is a Software engineer and Chief Operating Officer (COO) at Nexton, based in Buenos Aires, Argentina. He joined Nexton in 2019 and leads operations, talent strategy and revenue operations, connecting Latin American engineers with US companies. He writes about technical hiring, distributed engineering teams and AI in business operations.
